About

ZapFloor is a smart workspace operating system that helps coworking spaces, flexible office providers, and corporate campuses manage their business end-to-end. The platform combines advanced online booking, community engagement tools, and analytics dashboards — enabling operators to optimize occupancy, streamline administration, and build thriving member communities. Clients pay a recurring subscription fee, providing a predictable revenue model that scales with workspace size.
